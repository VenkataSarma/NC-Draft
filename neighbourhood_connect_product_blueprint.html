01 · Project overview

What is Neighbourhood Connect?

Neighbourhood Connect is a hyperlocal social discovery and community platform. It helps people discover others around them, find people with shared interests, make plans and participate in things happening in their neighbourhood.

The starting point is simple: people may live close to one another, but they often do not know each other. The app makes it easier to turn that physical proximity into meaningful social connection and collective action.

Core idea: Move social interaction beyond likes, followers and endless scrolling. Help people meet, participate, collaborate and get things done in the real world.

Initial target market: Hyderabad, India, with the product designed to expand to other neighbourhoods and cities.

02 · The problem

People are nearby, but communities are disconnected

Problem 01

Finding people to do things with

Someone wants to go for a morning walk, play cricket, learn guitar or watch a movie, but cannot find a suitable group nearby.

Problem 02

Digital connection without real connection

People spend time on social platforms following content and collecting interactions, but may not build relationships with people living around them.

Problem 03

Local information is scattered

Neighbourhood updates, local problems, event announcements and requests for help are spread across separate chats and informal groups.

Problem 04

Newcomers and travellers lack a local network

People visiting or moving to a place may want company, local suggestions or a way to join activities, but do not know whom to approach.

Why existing platforms do not fully solve it

04 · Core user experience

How the application works

Register→Build profile→Choose interests→Discover nearby→Join or create→Connect and participate

Instant join: no approval friction

The intended default experience is simple: if an activity has available places and is open to the community, a person can join immediately. No organiser approval request and no waiting for acceptance. The goal is to make participation feel as easy as saying, “I'm in.”

Product rule: Open activities use instant joining. Show the available capacity and current participant count clearly, and close joining when the activity is full or no longer available.

For the current product direction, keep the joining journey lightweight. Private or restricted participation can be considered later only if a real use case requires it; it should not complicate the basic community experience.

Example journey

  1. A resident posts: “Cricket at 6 PM, need 4 more players.”
  2. Nearby users who are interested discover the activity.
  3. They tap Join and are added immediately if spaces remain.
  4. The group uses activity chat to coordinate the location and timing.
  5. After the activity, participants can stay connected and discover another shared activity.

What users can do

Nearby activity discovery

Find sports, fitness, learning, entertainment, hobby and social activities using location, category, date and availability.

Create an activity

Post what you want to do, when, where, the number of participants needed and useful details.

06 · Neighbour Connect + Travellers

Connect residents and people on the move

Travellers should be part of the community experience, not a disconnected travel product. Someone visiting Hyderabad, Goa or another city should be able to discover local people, activities and shared plans.

Find travel companions

Discover people planning a similar trip, route or destination and connect around compatible plans.

Join local activities while travelling

A visitor can find a cricket game, food walk, sightseeing plan, local event or group outing near their stay.

Share travel plans

Post destination, dates, interests and the kind of company or activity being sought.

Local community discovery

Help visitors discover community events and connect with residents who choose to participate.

Important design consideration: Travel connections involve meeting people who may not know each other. Add clear profile context, reporting/blocking, meetup guidance and privacy controls before promoting this feature widely.
07 · Product evolution

What comes next — version by version

Build a useful activity network first. Then expand it into a platform that supports the wider needs of a neighbourhood. AI should strengthen a useful community, not be added before the community has meaningful activity and data.

Foundation / V1 — Make local connections happen Core product

  • Registration, login and user profiles.
  • Interest and location-based discovery.
  • Create, discover and instantly join open activities.
  • Nearby people discovery and connections.
  • Direct and activity group messaging.
  • Notifications, reports, blocking and moderation.
  • Complete the web experience and connect the key screens to the backend.

Next version — Neighbourhood community layer Planned

  • Community discussions: topic-based conversations for residents and local groups.
  • Report local issues: raise problems such as garbage collection, streetlights, water supply, damaged roads or public-space maintenance.
  • Issue status and updates: allow the community to add details, discuss possible solutions and follow progress.
  • Gatherings and announcements: organise festivals, clean-up drives, society meetings, sports days and informal get-togethers.
  • Community participation: comment, support, share updates and volunteer where appropriate.

This version changes the product from “find someone to do something with” into “participate in what is happening around your neighbourhood.”

Following version — Community leadership Planned

  • Introduce community leaders or moderators for a defined neighbourhood or community.
  • Give leaders tools to organise gatherings, publish verified announcements and coordinate volunteers.
  • Provide issue-management tools to track reports, assign follow-up and post progress updates.
  • Give residents a transparent way to identify community representatives and understand their role.

Define the role carefully: a community leader should help coordinate and moderate, not have unchecked control over residents or discussions.

Next stage — AI community assistant Planned

  • Introduce an AI chatbot that can answer questions using relevant information from the application.
  • Help users find activities, people, discussions, gatherings and local issue updates.
  • Answer natural-language questions such as “What is happening near me this weekend?” or “Has anyone reported the garbage issue in my area?”
  • Summarise long discussions and surface useful information without making users search through every post.
  • Provide source links or references to the posts and updates used in an answer.

Intelligence layer — Personalised recommendations Planned

  • Recommend activities based on interests, past participation and saved preferences.
  • Suggest people with overlapping interests and compatible activity patterns.
  • Recommend gatherings and discussions relevant to the user's neighbourhood.
  • Learn from meaningful signals such as joins, attendance or completion, saves and feedback—not just clicks.
  • Improve relevance over time while allowing users to control personalisation.
08 · AI and recommendation system

Make the platform feel relevant to each person

A. AI chatbot: understand the whole community

The assistant should work as a natural-language interface over approved application data. It can help a user discover information that already exists in activities, profiles, discussions, gatherings and issue reports.

User asksAssistant should help find
“What can I do this evening nearby?”Open activities matching location, time and interests.
“Are people playing badminton this weekend?”Relevant badminton activities and their availability.
“What is happening in my community?”Upcoming gatherings, announcements and active discussions.
“Has anyone raised the garbage issue?”Relevant issue reports, discussion and latest status.
“How can I meet people who like photography?”Relevant public profiles, groups and activities, subject to privacy settings.
Implementation direction: Use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over permission-filtered application content. Retrieve relevant records, generate a grounded answer, and link back to the original content. The chatbot must respect visibility, privacy and access permissions; it should never expose private messages or hidden profile data to unauthorised users.

B. Recommendation engine: predict what may be useful

The recommendation engine should learn from a user's interests and actual participation history to surface activities and connections they are more likely to find valuable.

Possible input signals

  • Selected interests and skills
  • Activities joined and attended
  • Categories explored or saved
  • Preferred days, times and distance
  • Feedback and repeat participation

Possible recommendations

  • Activities you may want to join
  • People with shared interests
  • Upcoming gatherings nearby
  • Relevant community discussions
  • New interests and activities to explore

Suggested recommendation pipeline

Collect consented signals→Build user-interest profile→Generate candidates→Rank by relevance→Show recommendations→Learn from feedback

First approach: Start with a transparent rule-based scoring model using interest overlap, distance, time compatibility, activity availability and previous participation. Later, evaluate collaborative filtering or learning-to-rank methods once enough quality interaction data exists.

Example scoring dimensions: shared-interest score, location suitability, time fit, activity popularity/availability and personal participation history. Keep the score explainable—for example, “Recommended because you joined two weekend cricket activities.”

Do not optimise only for engagement. A good recommendation should help people find a meaningful activity or connection, not keep them endlessly scrolling. Avoid sensitive inferences, provide personalisation controls and measure real participation outcomes.
09 · Trust, privacy and safety

Community growth needs responsible design

  • Location privacy: use approximate public location and distance-based discovery; do not expose a user's precise home coordinates.
  • User control: provide profile visibility and discoverability settings, plus block and report controls.
  • Safe meetups: publish practical meetup guidance, public-place suggestions and optional check-in or trusted-contact features.
  • Moderation: support reporting of users, activities, discussions and issue posts, with clear review workflows.
  • AI permissions: retrieve only content the requesting user is authorised to see; keep private conversations private.
  • Community leadership: define scope, accountability and escalation processes for leaders and moderators.
  • Instant-join safeguards: enforce capacity limits atomically, prevent duplicate joins and show clear activity status.

12 · How to measure success

Measure real community value

Do not judge the product only by registrations, impressions or time spent in the app. Measure whether people are actually connecting and participating.

Activity health

Activities created, join conversion, fill rate, attendance or completion rate, repeat participation.

Social connection

New connections formed, repeat interactions, group participation and connections that lead to activities.

Community usefulness

Issues reported, discussions with constructive participation, gatherings organised and updates resolved or closed.

Recommendation quality

Recommendation-to-join rate, attendance after recommendation, user feedback and diversity of activities discovered.

13 · Explain the project simply

The one-minute project explanation

“Neighbourhood Connect is a community-driven platform that helps people build real connections with the people around them.”

Today, many of us live in the same neighbourhood but barely know the people around us. We use social media to follow people, watch content and share posts, but it does not always help us build an actual social life.

With Neighbourhood Connect, people can find others nearby who share their interests, create activities like cricket, running, learning or movie plans, and join instantly. Travellers can also connect with local people and discover activities in the places they visit.

Over time, the platform will grow into a complete community space where residents can discuss local problems, report issues like garbage collection, organise gatherings and work together. Community leaders can help coordinate these efforts, while AI can make it easier to find relevant information, people and activities.

The long-term goal is simple: use technology not just to connect people online, but to help them build better communities offline.

A useful product test

What should a user be able to say?

“I found people to play with. I joined a local event. I met someone with the same interests. I found out what was happening in my neighbourhood. I helped get a local issue noticed.”

If Neighbourhood Connect consistently enables those outcomes, it is delivering on its purpose.
